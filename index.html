Travel & Transport Guide

Tube & Train Station Statuses
💡 TfL Top Tip: For the easiest and least crowded journey, take the Tube or Elizabeth Line to Paddington Station, Kensal Rise, or Shepherd's Bush and follow the signed walking route.
⛔ Ladbroke Grove Station CLOSED ALL DAY

Closed all day on both Sunday and Monday for safety.

⚠️ Notting Hill Gate Station NO ENTRY 11:00 - 18:00

No entry between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM. District & Circle lines will not stop, and there is no interchange between Central and District/Circle lines.

⚠️ Westbourne Park Station EXIT ONLY from 11:00

Exit only from 11:00 AM onwards. No entry permitted.

⚠️ Holland Park Station CLOSES 15:00

No entry after 11:00 AM. Station closes completely at 15:00.

⚠️ Queensway Station NO ENTRY 11:00 - 18:00

No entry between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Exit only.

⚠️ Royal Oak Station CLOSES 18:00

No entry after 11:00 AM. Station closes completely at 18:00.

⚠️ Latimer Road Station CLOSES 23:00

Closes at 23:00. May also close temporarily during busy periods.

⚠️ Shepherd's Bush RESTRICTED FROM 16:00

No ticket issuing facilities and no down escalator after 16:00.

⚠️ Kensal Rise & Kensal Green QUEUES EXPECTED

Kensal Green queues expected outside. Kensal Rise Chamberlayne Road entrance closes at 18:00 with queuing via Station Terrace.

🟢 Recommended Hub: Paddington Station OPEN / RECOMMENDED

Major interchange hub for Elizabeth Line, National Rail, Bakerloo, Circle, District, and Hammersmith & City lines. Short signed walk directly to Carnival.

Bus Services & Diversions
🚍 Bus Diversions: Buses operate from two main hubs outside the carnival perimeter: Prince of Wales (Harrow Road) in the north, and Notting Hill Gate in the south.
🚌 Special Express Bus 148X 10:00 – 22:00
📍 Notting Hill Gate ↔ Victoria & Vauxhall

Limited-stop express service running between Notting Hill Gate and Victoria (10:00 - 17:00), then extending to Vauxhall until 22:00.

🚌 Special Express Bus 7X Daytime
📍 Oxford Circus ↔ Paddington ↔ Du Cane Road ↔ East Acton

Direct limited-stop express service connects central London with East Acton via Paddington.

🚌 Special Express Bus 18X 12:00 – 22:00
📍 Prince of Wales (Harrow Rd) ↔ Warren Street ↔ Stonebridge Park

Runs between Warren Street and Stonebridge Park via Prince of Wales pub hub.

🚌 Special Express Bus 36X Daytime
📍 Peckham ➔ Harrow Road (One Direction Only)

Runs one-way from Peckham and South London to Harrow Road.

🚌 Special Express Bus 23X Monday Afternoon Only
📍 Harrow Road (Prince of Wales) ↔ Victoria

Four special trips running on Monday afternoon only.
